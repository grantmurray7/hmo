One of the first practical steps in any planning-related campaign is to keep a dated record.

Residents are therefore beginning an observations log that can later support a more complete public timeline. The aim is not to speculate, but to note developments in an orderly way and match them against planning information where possible.

Entries in this timeline may eventually include:

- visible changes or works at the property
- relevant correspondence sent or received
- planning references or committee documents
- points requiring clarification from the local authority

Keeping an organised record helps avoid confusion and allows later updates to be more accurate.
