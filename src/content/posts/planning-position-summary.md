Residents involved in this campaign currently understand that Number 27 has permission for a **6 metre rear extension**.

That is the starting point for all public discussion on this site.

The concern being raised by neighbours is different. It is not simply that building work is taking place, but that the wider pattern of works or intended use may suggest an **HMO arrangement that would require separate planning scrutiny or permission**.

This distinction matters.

- An approved extension is one issue
- A possible change of use or intensified residential use is another

For that reason, this site will continue to describe the extension permission as an understood fact while treating any HMO-related point as a concern that still needs evidence and confirmation.

As more information becomes available, this summary can be refined with references to planning documents, correspondence, or official responses.
