Wilton Road Campaign has been launched as a straightforward public record for residents concerned about development activity at Number 27, Wilton Road, Redhill, RH1 6QR.

The immediate purpose of the site is simple: keep the story organised. That means placing updates in date order, identifying what is confirmed, and clearly marking what remains a concern or open question.

At the time of launch, residents understand that permission exists for a 6 metre rear extension. At the same time, there is concern that the works may point toward an HMO-related use or layout that has not been openly confirmed.

This website is not intended to make unverified claims as fact. It is intended to:

- document the issue carefully
- publish new developments in a consistent format
- create a record that neighbours, councillors, and interested readers can follow

Future updates will add more detail, source references, and a fuller timeline as information is gathered.
